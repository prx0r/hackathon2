# Hackathon Strategy — What Actually Wins

*Compiled from r/hackathon, TAIKAI, and real winning teams*

---

## The 6 judging criteria (TAIKAI framework)

| # | Criterion | What judges look for |
|---|-----------|---------------------|
| 1 | **Creativity & innovation** | Originality, fresh perspective, unexpected approach |
| 2 | **Technical execution** | Code quality, architecture, performance, right tech stack |
| 3 | **Functional MVP** | Core features work, demonstrates viability, proof of concept |
| 4 | **Problem-solving & relevance** | Solves the actual problem, audience fit, feasibility |
| 5 | **Impact & potential** | Real-world adoption, economic viability, sustainability |
| 6 | **Final pitch** | Vision clarity, holistic evaluation, compelling narrative |

## What winning teams actually do

### From r/hackathon (real examples)

1. **Talk to judges first.** A team spent 2 hours talking to judges informally before building. They figured out what judges actually cared about, then built directly for that. Demo was 3 slides + working prototype. Won by a mile.

2. **Simpler idea, insane execution.** Pick something you can demo perfectly in 5 minutes rather than something ambitious that barely works.

3. **Demo + storytelling > product depth.** Judges evaluate how well you explain, not just what you built.

4. **Start building before the event.** Having infrastructure ready means you spend hackathon time on the unique value, not boilerplate.

5. **Think like a business, not a developer.** Working demo + solid business model beats perfect product every time. B2B + B2C positioning, roadmap, competitor analysis made the difference.

### From TAIKAI (structured advice)

6. **3 slides + working demo.** Problem statement → demo → solution explanation. End in 5 minutes.

7. **Don't explain the idea forever.** Start with problem statement, start demo, explain while demoing.

8. **Implementation matters.** Good presentation with bad implementation loses. "1999 type UI/UX" loses.

9. **Build, don't Figma.** Directly go for the build — it's way faster and more convincing.

## Our specific strategy (OpenAIRE hackathon)

### What judges care about (from their rubric)

| Criterion | Score target | How we score |
|-----------|-------------|--------------|
| MCP connector | 5/5 | Live Alien trace, 11 tool calls, 5 IDs |
| Usefulness | 5/5 | Living reviews + AI agents need this |
| Originality | 5/5 | No existing end-to-end tool |
| Responsible use | 5/5 | Anti-cheat, frozen acceptance |
| Reproducibility | 5/5 | 33 tests, deterministic, 1 dependency |
| Clarity | 5/5 | One question, one loop, one demo |
